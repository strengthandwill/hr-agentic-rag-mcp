# Annual Leave (PTO) Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PTO-01
**Category:** Leave & Time Off
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies to:** All CivicPulse Digital Agency (CPDA) confirmed employees (Singapore)

> CPDA is a fictional Singapore government-technology agency created for this course project.
> All policies, figures, and data in this document are synthetic and for educational use only.

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Annual Leave, commonly referred to as Paid Time Off (PTO), accrues,
how it may be used, and how requests are approved at CivicPulse Digital Agency (CPDA).

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlement

Entitlement depends on employment type and length of service:

| Employment Type | Years of Service | Annual Leave (days/year) |
|---|---|---|
| Full-time, confirmed | 0–1 | 15 |
| Full-time, confirmed | 2–4 | 18 |
| Full-time, confirmed | 5+ | 21 |
| Part-time (>= 0.5 FTE) | Any | Pro-rated from full-time rate |
| Contractor / intern | Any | Not eligible under this policy; see contract terms |

Leave accrues monthly in equal instalments from the employee's start date and is credited to the
employee's leave balance in the HR system. New hires accrue from their confirmed start date;
there is no leave accrual during the first 3 months of probation, though probationary staff may
still request unpaid leave via their manager.

## 3. Requesting Annual Leave

1. Employees should submit leave requests at least **3 working days** in advance for leave of
   1–2 days, and at least **2 weeks** in advance for leave of 3 or more consecutive days.
2. Requests are submitted to the employee's direct manager for approval. Manager approval is
   **required** before leave is considered confirmed, regardless of remaining balance.
3. Leave taken without an approved request is treated as unpaid leave and may be subject to
   HR review under the Workplace Conduct Code (POL-CONDUCT-09).
4. Employees should always confirm their current PTO balance before submitting a request, since
   balances are updated monthly and may differ from personal estimates.

## 4. Carry-Forward and Encashment

- Up to **7 unused Annual Leave days** may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any
  balance above 7 days that is unused by 31 January of the following year is forfeited.
- Employees may encash up to 5 unused Annual Leave days per year at their basic daily rate,
  subject to manager and HR approval. Encashment requests are processed quarterly.

## 5. Leave During Notice Period

Employees serving their resignation notice period may take Annual Leave only with explicit
manager approval, and such leave will offset against the notice period only if agreed in writing
by HR.

## 6. Half-Day and Hourly Leave

Employees may request Annual Leave in half-day units (morning or afternoon) for local
appointments or short personal matters. Leave in smaller increments than half a day is not
tracked separately; employees should instead use the flexi-time provisions of the Remote Work &
FWA Policy (POL-FWA-03) for brief absences such as a medical appointment of under two hours.

## 7. Leave Cancellation and Changes

Approved leave may be cancelled or rescheduled by the employee at any time before the leave
starts, subject to manager notification. Once leave has started, cancelling the remaining portion
requires manager approval and should be requested through the HR system so the balance is
corrected accurately. Repeated last-minute cancellations may be raised by a manager as a
performance or planning concern, handled under ordinary management discretion rather than this
policy.

## 8. Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Standard request.** An employee with 3 years of service (18 days/year
entitlement) wants to take a single Friday off in three weeks. Because this is a 1-day request,
they need only 3 working days' notice; submitting 3 weeks ahead is well within policy and the
manager can approve at their discretion based on team coverage.

**Example 2 — Insufficient balance.** An employee has 1.5 days of Annual Leave remaining and
requests 5 consecutive days. The request cannot be approved as ordinary Annual Leave because the
balance is insufficient; the employee may take the available 1.5 days as Annual Leave and request
the remaining days as unpaid leave (manager discretion) or wait until the next month's accrual
increases the balance.

**Example 3 — Carry-forward at year-end.** An employee ends the calendar year with 9 unused
Annual Leave days. Up to 7 days carry forward into the new year (usable until 31 January); the
remaining 2 days are forfeited unless the employee successfully encashes them before year-end
under Section 4.

**Example 4 — Leave spanning a public holiday.** An employee requests 5 consecutive working days
of Annual Leave that happen to include a gazetted public holiday in the middle of the range. Per
the Public Holidays Policy (POL-HOL-02), the holiday itself is not deducted from the Annual
Leave balance — only 4 days are drawn down even though 5 calendar working days are "away."

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does Annual Leave accrue while I am on maternity or paternity leave?**
A: Yes. Family & Caregiving Leave (POL-FAM-10) runs on a separate balance and does not pause
Annual Leave accrual, which continues based on your confirmed employment status.

**Q: I am a part-time employee at 0.6 FTE. How is my entitlement calculated?**
A: Your entitlement is pro-rated from the applicable full-time tier. For example, an employee at
the 2–4 years tier (18 days full-time) working 0.6 FTE would accrue 18 × 0.6 = 10.8 days/year,
rounded per HR system convention.

**Q: Can I take Annual Leave on my first day after confirmation, before any days have accrued?**
A: No. Leave can only be taken up to the balance actually accrued and credited in the HR system
at the time of the request; accrual is monthly, not available in advance.

**Q: What happens if my manager does not respond to a leave request in time?**
A: If there is no response within 3 working days, escalate per the Manager Approval &
Escalation Workflow (POL-APPROVAL-12), which routes the request to the skip-level manager.

**Q: Can unused Annual Leave be paid out when I resign?**
A: Yes, any unused and accrued Annual Leave balance at the time of resignation is paid out as
part of final settlement, subject to standard payroll processing timelines; this is separate
from the in-service encashment process in Section 4, which is capped at 5 days/year.

**Q: Does sick leave convert into Annual Leave if I was scheduled to be on vacation but fell
ill?**
A: If an employee is hospitalised or has a medical certificate for the period of pre-approved
Annual Leave, the overlapping days may be reclassified as Medical Leave on request to People &
Culture, restoring the corresponding Annual Leave balance.
