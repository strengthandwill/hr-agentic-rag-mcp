# Public Holidays Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HOL-02
**Category:** Leave & Time Off
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies to:** All CPDA employees (Singapore)

> Fictional company policy created for this course project. Synthetic data only.

## 1. Gazetted Public Holidays (2026)

CPDA observes all gazetted public holidays declared by the Singapore Ministry of Manpower.
For 2026, these are:

| Holiday | Date |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | 1 January |
| Chinese New Year (Day 1 & 2) | 17–18 February |
| Good Friday | 3 April |
| Hari Raya Puasa | 20 March |
| Labour Day | 1 May |
| Vesak Day | 31 May |
| Hari Raya Haji | 27 May |
| National Day | 9 August |
| Deepavali | 8 November |
| Christmas Day | 25 December |

Dates are illustrative for this fictional exercise and follow the general Singapore public
holiday calendar pattern; employees should always verify against the official MOM calendar in a
real workplace.

## 2. Holiday Pay

Public holidays do not draw down an employee's Annual Leave balance. Full-time and part-time
(pro-rated) employees receive their usual pay for each gazetted holiday that falls on a working
day.

## 3. Holiday Falling on a Rest Day

If a public holiday falls on an employee's rest day (typically Saturday or Sunday for a standard
5-day work week), the next working day is observed as a paid holiday in lieu, per standard
Singapore employment practice.

## 4. Working on a Public Holiday

Employees who are required to work on a gazetted public holiday (e.g. for on-call incident
response) are entitled to either:

- An extra day's pay at the basic daily rate, **or**
- A replacement day off within 30 days, taken with manager approval.

The choice is at the employee's discretion and should be confirmed with the manager at the time
the work is assigned.
