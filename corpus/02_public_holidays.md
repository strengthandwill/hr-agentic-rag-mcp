# Public Holidays Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HOL-02
**Category:** Leave & Time Off
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies to:** All CPDA employees (Singapore)

> Fictional company policy created for this course project. Synthetic data only.

## 1. Gazetted Public Holidays (2026)

CPDA observes all gazetted public holidays declared by the Singapore Ministry of Manpower.
For 2026, these are:

| Holiday | Date |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | 1 January |
| Chinese New Year (Day 1 & 2) | 17–18 February |
| Good Friday | 3 April |
| Hari Raya Puasa | 20 March |
| Labour Day | 1 May |
| Vesak Day | 31 May |
| Hari Raya Haji | 27 May |
| National Day | 9 August |
| Deepavali | 8 November |
| Christmas Day | 25 December |

Dates are illustrative for this fictional exercise and follow the general Singapore public
holiday calendar pattern; employees should always verify against the official MOM calendar in a
real workplace.

## 2. Holiday Pay

Public holidays do not draw down an employee's Annual Leave balance. Full-time and part-time
(pro-rated) employees receive their usual pay for each gazetted holiday that falls on a working
day.

## 3. Holiday Falling on a Rest Day

If a public holiday falls on an employee's rest day (typically Saturday or Sunday for a standard
5-day work week), the next working day is observed as a paid holiday in lieu, per standard
Singapore employment practice.

## 4. Working on a Public Holiday

Employees who are required to work on a gazetted public holiday (e.g. for on-call incident
response) are entitled to either:

- An extra day's pay at the basic daily rate, **or**
- A replacement day off within 30 days, taken with manager approval.

The choice is at the employee's discretion and should be confirmed with the manager at the time
the work is assigned.

## 5. Regional and Office-Specific Holidays

CPDA's single Singapore HQ observes the national public holiday calendar listed in Section 1.
Employees temporarily working from another country under the Remote Work & FWA Policy
(POL-FWA-03) remain on the Singapore holiday calendar for pay and leave purposes; they are not
automatically entitled to the public holidays of the country they are visiting. An employee who
wishes to observe a local holiday in the country they are visiting instead should request Annual
Leave or a flexi-time adjustment for that day.

## 6. Half-Day Public Holidays

CPDA does not currently designate any half-day public holidays; all gazetted holidays in Section
1 are treated as full working-day equivalents for pay and leave-in-lieu purposes.

## 7. Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Holiday in lieu.** Chinese New Year Day 2 falls on a Thursday in the published
calendar; in a year where a gazetted holiday instead falls on a Sunday, the following Monday is
automatically credited as a paid day off in lieu — no action is required from the employee.

**Example 2 — Required to work a public holiday.** An engineer is asked to support a production
incident on National Day. They may choose either a day's extra pay at their basic daily rate or
a replacement day off within 30 days; this choice should be confirmed with their manager at the
time the on-call work is assigned, per Section 4.

**Example 3 — New hire starting the week of a holiday.** An employee starts on the Wednesday of
a week that includes a Friday public holiday. Because holiday pay is not pro-rated by tenure for
a day the employee is actually employed on, they receive the holiday as a normal paid day off.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are public holidays counted against my Annual Leave balance if I take the surrounding days
off as leave?**
A: No. Only the actual Annual Leave days are deducted; the public holiday itself is never drawn
from the Annual Leave balance, as illustrated in the Annual Leave Policy's worked example
(POL-PTO-01 §8, Example 4).

**Q: I am a part-time employee working 3 days a week. Do I get paid for a public holiday that
falls on a day I don't normally work?**
A: Public holiday pay applies to days that are part of your normal working schedule. If the
holiday falls on a day you are not rostered to work, no substitute day or additional pay applies
for that specific holiday.

**Q: Can I "bank" a day-in-lieu for working a public holiday and use it months later?**
A: The replacement day off must be taken within 30 days of the public holiday worked, per
Section 4, to keep payroll and leave records reconciled within the same reporting period.

**Q: Does CPDA add any company-specific holidays beyond the gazetted public holidays?**
A: Not currently. Any company-wide closure beyond the gazetted calendar (for example, a
year-end office closure) would be separately announced by People & Culture and, if announced,
documented as an addendum to this policy rather than drawn from Annual Leave.
