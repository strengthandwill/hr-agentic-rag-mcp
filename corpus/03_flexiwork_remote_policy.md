# Remote Work & Flexible Work Arrangement (FWA) Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FWA-03
**Category:** Ways of Working
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People & Culture Team, in consultation with IT Security
**Applies to:** All CPDA employees (Singapore), subject to role eligibility

> Fictional company policy created for this course project, loosely inspired by publicly
> reported flexible-work terminology used by Singapore government agencies. Synthetic data only.

## 1. Overview

CPDA supports three complementary forms of flexible work arrangement ("FWA"), consistent with
common Singapore public-sector practice:

- **Flexi-place** — working from a location other than the primary office (home, another CPDA
  office, or, subject to Section 4, overseas).
- **Flexi-time** — staggered start/end times within core collaboration hours of 10:00–16:00 SGT.
- **Flexi-load** — reduced-hours or part-time arrangements, subject to separate HR agreement.

FWA eligibility depends on role. Engineering, product, design, People & Culture, and most
corporate functions are FWA-eligible. Roles requiring on-site presence (e.g. facilities,
on-site IT support, secure data-centre operations) are generally not eligible for flexi-place.

## 2. Standard Remote Work (Within Singapore)

Eligible employees may work from home or another local location up to **3 days per week**
without prior approval, provided they remain reachable during core hours and attend any meetings
their manager designates as in-person. Employees must maintain a secure home-working setup per
the Data Security & Acceptable Use Policy (POL-SEC-05).

## 3. Extended or Full Remote Weeks

Requests to work remotely for **more than 3 consecutive working days**, including a full remote
week, require the employee's manager's advance written approval and should be logged in the HR
system at least 5 working days ahead, except in emergencies.

## 4. Overseas Work (Cross-Border Remote Work)

Working from outside Singapore is treated as a distinct category from ordinary remote work
because it introduces tax, immigration, data-residency, and security considerations:

1. **Duration limit:** Overseas remote work is capped at **10 working days per calendar
   quarter**, and no more than **20 working days per calendar year**, per employee, regardless of
   destination, unless a formal International Remote Work exception is granted by both the
   employee's manager and People & Culture.
2. **Advance approval required:** All overseas remote work — even a single day — requires
   written manager approval **and** a completed Overseas Work Notification submitted at least
   **2 weeks** in advance, so that People & Culture and IT Security can review tax residency,
   data-protection, and access-control implications for the destination country.
3. **Six-weeks-or-longer requests:** Any request to work overseas for **6 weeks or longer** is
   treated as an **International Remote Work Exception**, not standard FWA. These are rare, must
   be approved by the employee's Head of Department and People & Culture jointly, typically
   require a review of local tax/payroll obligations, and are not guaranteed to be approved. Most
   day-to-day overseas remote requests should stay within the quarterly/annual caps in item 1.
4. **Security requirements:** Overseas remote work must use CPDA-managed devices with disk
   encryption and VPN enabled at all times; access to production systems containing personal
   data is restricted per POL-SEC-05 §3, and may be temporarily suspended for destinations on the
   IT Security watch list.
5. **Employee responsibility:** Employees are responsible for their own personal tax and
   immigration compliance while working overseas; CPDA's approval process only governs internal
   HR and security requirements and is not tax advice.

## 5. Approval Chain

Standard remote work (Section 2): no approval needed, informal notice to manager suffices.
Extended remote weeks (Section 3) and all overseas work (Section 4): manager approval required;
requests longer than 6 weeks overseas additionally require Head of Department + People & Culture
sign-off. See **Manager Approval & Escalation Workflow (POL-APPROVAL-12)** for routing when a
manager is unavailable.
