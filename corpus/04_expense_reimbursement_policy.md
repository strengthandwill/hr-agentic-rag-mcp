# Expense & Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-EXP-04
**Category:** Finance & Operations
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** Finance Team
**Applies to:** All CPDA employees (Singapore)

> Fictional company policy created for this course project. Synthetic data only.

## 1. General Principles

Expenses must be reasonable, business-related, and supported by an itemised receipt. Claims
should be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred via the Expense system, and
require manager approval before reimbursement is processed.

## 2. Home Office Equipment

Employees who are FWA-eligible (see POL-FWA-03) may claim a **one-time home office setup
allowance of up to SGD 500**, covering items such as an ergonomic chair, monitor, keyboard, or
desk. Conditions:

- Available once every 3 years per employee.
- Requires a single consolidated claim with itemised receipts; partial claims for smaller amounts
  are allowed but count toward the SGD 500 cap.
- A basic ergonomic chair or a monitor is reimbursable under this allowance; high-end furniture
  (e.g. standing desks over SGD 800, designer chairs) requires additional Head of Department
  approval and is capped at SGD 500 reimbursement regardless of actual item cost.
- Laptops and primary work computers are **not** claimed under this allowance — see Section 3.

## 3. Laptops and Computing Equipment

Primary work laptops, monitors issued for office use, and peripherals required for the role are
provided directly by CPDA IT as company assets (see Equipment & Asset Policy POL-EQP-08) and are
**not reimbursed as a personal expense claim**. Employees needing an upgraded or additional
laptop for legitimate business reasons should raise an IT Asset Request rather than an expense
claim.

## 4. Travel Expenses

- **Domestic travel:** Reasonable transport (taxi/ride-hail/public transport) and meal expenses
  incurred for business purposes (client meetings, inter-office travel) are reimbursable with
  receipts.
- **International travel:** Must be pre-approved by the employee's manager before booking.
  Economy class flights are standard for trips under 6 hours; premium economy may be approved by
  a Head of Department for longer flights. A per-diem of **SGD 80/day** covers meals and
  incidentals for approved international business trips; accommodation is booked separately
  through Finance-approved vendors or reimbursed at actual cost with receipts up to a reasonable
  cap communicated at approval time.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursable under this policy: alcohol for personal consumption, fines
or penalties (e.g. parking tickets), personal entertainment, upgrades to first/business class
without Head of Department approval, and any expense lacking an itemised receipt.

## 6. Compliance Checks

Finance and People & Culture periodically audit expense claims for compliance with this policy.
Claims that are ambiguous (e.g. mixed personal/business travel) should be flagged by the
submitting employee and will be reviewed manually rather than auto-approved.
