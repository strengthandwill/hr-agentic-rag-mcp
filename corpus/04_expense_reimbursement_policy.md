# Expense & Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-EXP-04
**Category:** Finance & Operations
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** Finance Team
**Applies to:** All CPDA employees (Singapore)

> Fictional company policy created for this course project. Synthetic data only.

## 1. General Principles

Expenses must be reasonable, business-related, and supported by an itemised receipt. Claims
should be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred via the Expense system, and
require manager approval before reimbursement is processed.

## 2. Home Office Equipment

Employees who are FWA-eligible (see POL-FWA-03) may claim a **one-time home office setup
allowance of up to SGD 500**, covering items such as an ergonomic chair, monitor, keyboard, or
desk. Conditions:

- Available once every 3 years per employee.
- Requires a single consolidated claim with itemised receipts; partial claims for smaller amounts
  are allowed but count toward the SGD 500 cap.
- A basic ergonomic chair or a monitor is reimbursable under this allowance; high-end furniture
  (e.g. standing desks over SGD 800, designer chairs) requires additional Head of Department
  approval and is capped at SGD 500 reimbursement regardless of actual item cost.
- Laptops and primary work computers are **not** claimed under this allowance — see Section 3.

## 3. Laptops and Computing Equipment

Primary work laptops, monitors issued for office use, and peripherals required for the role are
provided directly by CPDA IT as company assets (see Equipment & Asset Policy POL-EQP-08) and are
**not reimbursed as a personal expense claim**. Employees needing an upgraded or additional
laptop for legitimate business reasons should raise an IT Asset Request rather than an expense
claim.

## 4. Travel Expenses

- **Domestic travel:** Reasonable transport (taxi/ride-hail/public transport) and meal expenses
  incurred for business purposes (client meetings, inter-office travel) are reimbursable with
  receipts.
- **International travel:** Must be pre-approved by the employee's manager before booking.
  Economy class flights are standard for trips under 6 hours; premium economy may be approved by
  a Head of Department for longer flights. A per-diem of **SGD 80/day** covers meals and
  incidentals for approved international business trips; accommodation is booked separately
  through Finance-approved vendors or reimbursed at actual cost with receipts up to a reasonable
  cap communicated at approval time.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are never reimbursable under this policy: alcohol for personal consumption, fines
or penalties (e.g. parking tickets), personal entertainment, upgrades to first/business class
without Head of Department approval, and any expense lacking an itemised receipt.

## 6. Compliance Checks

Finance and People & Culture periodically audit expense claims for compliance with this policy.
Claims that are ambiguous (e.g. mixed personal/business travel) should be flagged by the
submitting employee and will be reviewed manually rather than auto-approved.

## 7. Client and Business Entertainment

Reasonable meal expenses for meetings with external vendors, partners, or job candidates are
reimbursable with an itemised receipt and a brief note of the business purpose and attendees.
A soft guideline of SGD 60 per person applies; amounts above this should be pre-approved by a
Head of Department.

## 8. Currency and Foreign Transactions

Expenses incurred in a foreign currency are reimbursed in SGD using the exchange rate shown on
the original receipt or card statement; employees do not need to manually convert amounts when
submitting a claim, as the Expense system performs this conversion automatically from the
uploaded receipt.

## 9. Approval Thresholds

| Claim Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to SGD 200 | Direct manager only |
| SGD 201–1,000 | Direct manager, then Finance review |
| Above SGD 1,000 | Direct manager, Head of Department, then Finance review |

Claims are typically reimbursed within 10 working days of final approval, via the employee's
registered payroll bank account.

## 10. Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Home office chair.** An employee who has not used the allowance in the past 3
years buys an ergonomic chair for SGD 320. This is a standard, reimbursable item under Section 2
and is well within the SGD 500 cap; a single claim with the itemised receipt is sufficient.

**Example 2 — Standing desk over the cap.** An employee buys a standing desk for SGD 750. Per
Section 2, this requires Head of Department approval and is reimbursed only up to SGD 500
regardless of the item's actual cost — the employee covers the remaining SGD 250 personally.

**Example 3 — Laptop upgrade request.** An employee wants a more powerful laptop for
video-rendering work. Per Section 3, this is not a personal expense claim; it should be raised as
an IT Asset Request to IT Operations, evaluated against business need rather than reimbursed
after a personal purchase.

**Example 4 — International conference trip.** An employee attends a 4-day overseas conference.
Economy flights and conference registration are pre-approved by their manager before booking;
accommodation is booked through a Finance-approved vendor; a SGD 80/day per-diem covers meals
and incidentals for the trip duration, submitted as a lump-sum claim with the approved travel
request attached.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I submit an expense claim more than 30 days after the expense was incurred?**
A: Claims submitted after 30 days may still be processed at Finance's discretion but are not
guaranteed; submitting promptly avoids any risk of the claim being declined for lateness.

**Q: Is a working lunch with my own team (no external guests) reimbursable?**
A: No. Internal team meals without an external business purpose are not reimbursable under this
policy; team-building budgets, where available, are managed separately by People & Culture.

**Q: My home office allowance claim was for SGD 450, under the SGD 500 cap, but 3 years have not
yet passed since my last claim. What happens?**
A: The SGD 500 allowance is available once every 3 years per Section 2, regardless of how much
of the previous allowance was used; a new claim before the 3-year mark is not eligible.

**Q: Do I need pre-approval for domestic taxi fares to a client meeting?**
A: No pre-approval is required for ordinary domestic transport within the approval thresholds in
Section 9; simply submit the claim with a receipt noting the business purpose.

**Q: What if my itemised receipt is lost?**
A: A claim without any supporting receipt is generally not reimbursable per Section 5. For a
lost receipt, check whether a card or bank statement plus a brief written explanation is
acceptable to Finance on a case-by-case basis; this is not guaranteed.
