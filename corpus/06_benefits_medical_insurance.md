# Benefits & Medical Insurance Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BEN-06
**Category:** Benefits
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies to:** CPDA employees (Singapore); eligibility varies by employment type

> Fictional company policy created for this course project. Synthetic data only.

## 1. Eligibility by Employment Type

| Employment Type | Medical & Dental | Life/Personal Accident Insurance | Wellness Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-time, confirmed | Full coverage | Covered | SGD 400/year |
| Full-time, probation | Emergency-only coverage | Covered | Not yet eligible |
| Part-time (>= 0.5 FTE) | Pro-rated coverage | Covered | SGD 200/year |
| Contractor | Not eligible (see contract) | Not eligible | Not eligible |
| Intern | Emergency-only coverage | Covered | Not eligible |

Confirmed full-time employees become eligible for full medical and dental coverage on their
confirmation date (typically after a 3-month probation period).

## 2. Medical Coverage

CPDA provides outpatient medical coverage (GP visits, specialist referrals) and hospitalisation
& surgical insurance for confirmed employees, administered through the CPDA-appointed insurer.
Dependents (spouse, children) may be added to dental and outpatient coverage at the employee's
own cost via payroll deduction; hospitalisation coverage for dependents is subsidised at 50%.

## 3. Dental Coverage

Confirmed employees receive an annual dental benefit of up to **SGD 300** for routine care
(cleaning, fillings) at panel clinics, or partial reimbursement for non-panel clinics.

## 4. Wellness Benefit

Confirmed full-time employees may claim up to **SGD 400 per calendar year** toward gym
memberships, fitness classes, or approved wellness apps. Part-time employees receive a
pro-rated SGD 200/year. This benefit does not carry forward between years.

## 5. CPF Contributions

For Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents, CPDA contributes to the Central Provident Fund
(CPF) at the prevailing statutory employer rate, in addition to the employee's own contribution
deducted from payroll, consistent with Singapore CPF Board requirements.

## 6. Benefits Status Changes

Life events (marriage, birth of a child, change in employment type) should be reported to
People & Culture within 30 days so that dependent coverage and benefits status can be updated.
Benefits questions specific to an individual's coverage should be verified against that
employee's current benefits election record, since coverage tiers can change after confirmation,
promotion, or a switch between full-time and part-time status.
