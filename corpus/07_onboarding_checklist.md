# New Hire Onboarding Checklist & Process

**Document ID:** POL-ONB-07
**Category:** Onboarding
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies to:** All new CPDA employees and their hiring managers

> Fictional company policy created for this course project. Synthetic data only.

## 1. Before Day 1 (HR & IT)

- People & Culture sends the offer letter, employment contract, and a welcome pack.
- IT provisions the employee's account, email, and prepares a laptop per the Equipment & Asset
  Policy (POL-EQP-08).
- The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the team.

## 2. Day 1

- Facilities badge issuance and office orientation (CPDA HQ, 88 Pasir Panjang Way, Harbourfront
  Tower, Singapore 118412 — fictional address for this exercise).
- IT hands over the laptop, sets up VPN and endpoint security agent per the Data Security &
  Acceptable Use Policy (POL-SEC-05).
- People & Culture briefing covering Annual Leave (POL-PTO-01), Public Holidays (POL-HOL-02),
  and how to enrol in Benefits (POL-BEN-06).

## 3. Week 1

- Benefits enrolment window opens; new hires have **14 days** from their start date to submit
  benefits elections (medical, dental, wellness) via the HR system.
- Manager sets up 1:1 cadence and shares team norms, including applicable Remote Work & FWA
  eligibility (POL-FWA-03) for the role.
- Mandatory Data Security awareness training (POL-SEC-05) must be completed within the first
  week.

## 4. First 30 Days

- Complete the Workplace Conduct Code acknowledgment (POL-CONDUCT-09).
- Meet with manager to set 30/60/90-day goals.
- Confirm reporting line and manager-of-record in the HR system, which determines leave and
  expense approval routing (see Manager Approval & Escalation Workflow, POL-APPROVAL-12).

## 5. Probation and Confirmation

- Standard probation period is 3 months for full-time hires.
- Annual Leave does not accrue during probation; full benefits activate on confirmation
  (POL-BEN-06 §1).
- Managers submit a confirmation review before the end of the probation period; extensions of up
  to 3 months are possible in consultation with People & Culture.

## 6. Related Policies

- **Equipment & Asset Policy (POL-EQP-08)** — device issuance and return.
- **Benefits & Medical Insurance Policy (POL-BEN-06)** — enrolment details.
- **Data Security & Acceptable Use Policy (POL-SEC-05)** — mandatory training.
