# New Hire Onboarding Checklist & Process

**Document ID:** POL-ONB-07
**Category:** Onboarding
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies to:** All new CPDA employees and their hiring managers

> Fictional company policy created for this course project. Synthetic data only.

## 1. Before Day 1 (HR & IT)

- People & Culture sends the offer letter, employment contract, and a welcome pack.
- IT provisions the employee's account, email, and prepares a laptop per the Equipment & Asset
  Policy (POL-EQP-08).
- The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the team.

## 2. Day 1

- Facilities badge issuance and office orientation (CPDA HQ, 88 Pasir Panjang Way, Harbourfront
  Tower, Singapore 118412 — fictional address for this exercise).
- IT hands over the laptop, sets up VPN and endpoint security agent per the Data Security &
  Acceptable Use Policy (POL-SEC-05).
- People & Culture briefing covering Annual Leave (POL-PTO-01), Public Holidays (POL-HOL-02),
  and how to enrol in Benefits (POL-BEN-06).

## 3. Week 1

- Benefits enrolment window opens; new hires have **14 days** from their start date to submit
  benefits elections (medical, dental, wellness) via the HR system.
- Manager sets up 1:1 cadence and shares team norms, including applicable Remote Work & FWA
  eligibility (POL-FWA-03) for the role.
- Mandatory Data Security awareness training (POL-SEC-05) must be completed within the first
  week.

## 4. First 30 Days

- Complete the Workplace Conduct Code acknowledgment (POL-CONDUCT-09).
- Meet with manager to set 30/60/90-day goals.
- Confirm reporting line and manager-of-record in the HR system, which determines leave and
  expense approval routing (see Manager Approval & Escalation Workflow, POL-APPROVAL-12).

## 5. Probation and Confirmation

- Standard probation period is 3 months for full-time hires.
- Annual Leave does not accrue during probation; full benefits activate on confirmation
  (POL-BEN-06 §1).
- Managers submit a confirmation review before the end of the probation period; extensions of up
  to 3 months are possible in consultation with People & Culture.

## 6. Role-Specific Onboarding

In addition to the general checklist above, certain roles have supplementary onboarding steps:

- **Engineering roles** complete a codebase and tooling orientation with their onboarding buddy
  in Week 1, and are provisioned with source-control and cloud-environment access by IT
  Operations once the Data Security training (Section 3) is complete.
- **People-facing roles** (HR Business Partners, IT Support) complete an additional briefing on
  handling Restricted personal data, referencing the Data Security & Acceptable Use Policy
  (POL-SEC-05 §4) data classification standard.
- **Contractors** follow an abbreviated onboarding (Day 1 and Week 1 items only) since they are
  not eligible for benefits enrolment (POL-BEN-06 §1) or standard Annual Leave accrual
  (POL-PTO-01 §2).

## 7. Buddy Program

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy — a peer on the same team, not their manager —
for the first 90 days. The buddy's role is informal day-to-day support (navigating tools,
explaining unwritten team norms, answering quick questions) and is distinct from the formal
30/60/90-day goal-setting process, which remains the manager's responsibility.

## 8. Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Engineering new hire, Week 1.** A new Software Engineer completes IT provisioning
and badge issuance on Day 1, Data Security training by end of Week 1 (Section 3), and receives
source-control access from IT Operations immediately after training completion, consistent with
Section 6's role-specific requirement.

**Example 2 — Late benefits enrolment.** A new hire forgets to submit benefits elections within
the 14-day window (Section 3). People & Culture should be contacted immediately; a late
enrolment may still be processed at HR's discretion, but coverage typically only begins from
the date of actual enrolment, not retroactively to the start date.

**Example 3 — Contractor onboarding.** A contractor joining for a 6-month engagement completes
Day 1 facility and IT provisioning and the Data Security training, but skips the benefits
enrolment and People & Culture leave briefing steps, consistent with their non-eligibility under
POL-PTO-01 §2 and POL-BEN-06 §1.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What happens if my probation period needs to be extended?**
A: Per Section 5, extensions of up to 3 months are possible in consultation with People &
Culture; your manager will discuss the reasons and expectations with you before the original
probation end date.

**Q: Who do I contact if my IT equipment is not ready on Day 1?**
A: Contact IT Operations directly, and loop in your manager; Day 1 provisioning gaps are handled
as a priority since they block the rest of the Week 1 onboarding checklist.

**Q: Is the onboarding buddy the same as my manager for probation review purposes?**
A: No. The buddy (Section 7) provides informal peer support; your manager remains responsible
for formal probation review and confirmation decisions (Section 5).

**Q: When do I get access to internal systems that contain employee personal data?**
A: Only after completing the Data Security training (Section 3) and, for people-facing roles,
the supplementary Restricted-data briefing in Section 6 — access is not granted before training
completion regardless of role urgency.
