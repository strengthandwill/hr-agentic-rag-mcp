# Equipment & Asset Policy

**Document ID:** POL-EQP-08
**Category:** IT & Operations
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** IT Operations Team
**Applies to:** All CPDA employees issued company equipment

> Fictional company policy created for this course project. Synthetic data only.

## 1. Standard Issue

New employees are issued a standard laptop (role-dependent spec: engineering roles receive a
higher-spec developer laptop), a monitor for office use, and standard peripherals. All equipment
remains CPDA property.

## 2. Taking Equipment Off-Site

Laptops may be taken home or travelled with for remote/flexi-place work under the Remote Work &
FWA Policy (POL-FWA-03). Monitors and other office peripherals are intended for office use and
require manager approval to be relocated permanently to a home setup.

## 3. Overseas Travel with Equipment

When travelling overseas with CPDA equipment (business trip or approved overseas remote work),
employees must ensure disk encryption and VPN are active per the Data Security & Acceptable Use
Policy (POL-SEC-05), and should notify IT Operations of the destination country in advance so
that any region-specific access restrictions can be applied.

## 4. Loss, Damage, or Theft

Loss, damage, or theft of CPDA equipment must be reported to IT Operations and IT Security
within 24 hours. First-time accidental loss/damage is generally covered by CPDA; repeated
incidents may result in a partial replacement cost being charged to the employee, at HR's
discretion.

## 5. Asset Return

All CPDA equipment must be returned to IT Operations on or before the employee's last working
day. Final pay may be withheld pending equipment return, consistent with standard employment
practice.

## 6. Requesting Additional or Upgraded Equipment

Employees needing equipment beyond standard issue (e.g. a second monitor for home use, a
specialised peripheral) should submit an IT Asset Request with manager approval; this is
separate from the personal expense claim process described in the Expense & Reimbursement
Policy (POL-EXP-04) §3.

## 7. Asset Inventory and Refresh Cycle

IT Operations maintains an inventory of all issued equipment by serial number against the
employee's HR record. Standard laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or sooner if a hardware
fault cannot be economically repaired. Employees do not need to request a refresh; IT Operations
proactively schedules replacements based on the inventory.

## 8. Personal Use of Company Equipment

Incidental personal use of a CPDA laptop (e.g. personal email, light browsing) is permitted under
the same terms as personal use of CPDA accounts generally (Data Security & Acceptable Use Policy,
POL-SEC-05 §5). Installing personal software that is not relevant to work, or using the device
for file-sharing/torrenting, is not permitted.

## 9. Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Coffee spill.** An employee accidentally spills coffee on their laptop keyboard.
This is reported to IT Operations and IT Security within 24 hours per Section 4; as a first-time
accidental incident, repair or replacement is generally covered by CPDA at no cost to the
employee.

**Example 2 — Repeated loss.** An employee loses their second CPDA-issued phone within a year.
Per Section 4, repeated incidents may result in a partial replacement cost being charged to the
employee, at HR's discretion, taking into account the circumstances of each loss.

**Example 3 — Relocating a monitor home permanently.** An employee who has shifted to a mostly
remote schedule wants to keep their office monitor at home full-time rather than carrying it back
and forth. Per Section 2, this requires manager approval to formally relocate office peripherals,
distinct from simply taking a laptop home under standard remote-work provisions.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I buy my old laptop from CPDA when it's due for refresh?**
A: Decommissioned equipment is securely wiped and disposed of or redeployed internally per IT
Operations' asset lifecycle process; employee purchase of retired equipment is not currently
offered under this policy.

**Q: Do I need to return equipment if I'm only going on extended leave, not resigning?**
A: No. Equipment return (Section 5) applies at the end of employment. Equipment may be retained
during approved leave unless IT Operations requests a temporary return for a refresh cycle or
security reason.

**Q: What if my CPDA laptop is damaged while travelling overseas for business?**
A: Report it to IT Operations and IT Security within 24 hours per Section 4, same as any other
damage; note any destination-specific security considerations from Section 3 when reporting so
IT Security can assess whether data may have been exposed.
