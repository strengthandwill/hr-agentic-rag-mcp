# Family & Caregiving Leave Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FAM-10
**Category:** Leave & Time Off
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies to:** All CPDA employees (Singapore); eligibility varies by leave type

> Fictional company policy created for this course project, loosely informed by publicly
> reported Singapore public-sector leave categories (e.g. child care, compassionate, shared
> parental, marriage leave). Synthetic data only.

## 1. Leave Types Summary

| Leave Type | Eligibility | Entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| Maternity Leave | Confirmed female employees | 16 weeks (per Singapore government-paid maternity leave norms) |
| Paternity Leave | Confirmed male employees, child born after marriage | 4 weeks |
| Shared Parental Leave | Eligible parents, by mutual agreement | Up to 4 weeks, shared from maternity entitlement |
| Child Care Leave | Parents with children under 7 | 6 days/year (confirmed employees) |
| Extended Child Care Leave | Parents with children aged 7–12 | 2 days/year |
| Marriage Leave | All confirmed employees | 3 days, once, within 3 months of marriage |
| Compassionate Leave | All employees | Up to 3 days per bereavement (immediate family) |
| Unpaid Infant Care Leave | Parents of children under 2 | Up to 6 days/year |

These entitlements are separate from, and do not draw down, the Annual Leave (PTO) balance
described in POL-PTO-01.

## 2. Maternity and Paternity Leave

Maternity Leave (16 weeks) may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected delivery date. Paternity
Leave (4 weeks) may be taken flexibly within 12 months of the child's birth, subject to manager
scheduling discussion. Both require supporting documentation (e.g. birth certificate or medical
certificate) submitted to People & Culture.

## 3. Shared Parental Leave

Eligible parents may agree to share up to 4 weeks of the mother's maternity entitlement with the
father, subject to joint written agreement submitted to People & Culture at least 4 weeks before
the intended start date.

## 4. Child Care and Extended Child Care Leave

Child Care Leave (6 days/year) applies to confirmed employees with at least one child under 7.
Extended Child Care Leave (2 days/year) applies for children aged 7 up to 12. These may be taken
in single days and do not need to be consecutive.

## 5. Marriage Leave

Confirmed employees receive 3 days of Marriage Leave, usable within 3 months of the marriage
date, on submission of a marriage certificate.

## 6. Compassionate Leave

Up to 3 days of paid Compassionate Leave is available per bereavement of an immediate family
member (spouse, parent, child, sibling). Additional unpaid leave may be granted by the manager
on a case-by-case basis.

## 7. Requesting Family & Caregiving Leave

Requests are submitted through the HR system to the employee's manager, similar to Annual Leave
requests (POL-PTO-01 §3), but are tracked against a separate leave balance category. Supporting
documentation should be provided within 14 days where applicable (birth certificate, marriage
certificate, death certificate, etc.).
